Good to know

Questions

What time do tours start?

Pick-up is at 8:30 am from your hotel lobby, and we're back around 5:00 pm.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, a dry bag and cash for souvenirs. We provide snorkels, life vests and lunch.

What if the weather is bad?

If the coast guard cancels trips, we'll rebook you for free or give a full refund.

Are tours kid-friendly?

Yes! Kids 4–11 get 30% off, and children under 4 join for free.
