Our story

It started with a kettle and a sidewalk.

In 2019, we set up two stools and a small coffee cart on the corner of our street. Neighbors came for the barako and stayed for the conversation.

Today the cart is a little café, but the idea hasn't changed: good local coffee, fair prices for our farmers, and a place where everyone knows your order.

— Lito & Joy, owners
